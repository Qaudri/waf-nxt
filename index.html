About

NEXTPlay is the world's first AI-native sports franchise, built as a vertically integrated ecosystem that introduces America's No. 1 sport to the women's sports landscape through a professional league NEXTPlay Pro, a next-generation flag football platform NEXTPlay Flag, and a youth development pathway NEXTPlay Futures.

NEXTPlay is leading sports into the AI era. Every critical decision—on and off the field—is guided by artificial intelligence in real time. Fans don't just watch games; they shape them, personalizing live broadcasts through interactive controls, real-time data layers, and AI-driven gaming insights.

Athletes receive real-time on-field guidance, coaches are supported with AI-assisted game planning and play-calling, officiating is continuously validated by AI, and the result is a faster, smarter, safer, and more immersive football experience built for the modern sports fan.

Gameplay Format

WAFN is a unique hybrid of Tackle Football and Flag Football in a 7-on-7 format. It features the intensity and physicality of tackle football x the speed and high scoring action of flag football.

Games will include four (4) quarters of action with each quarter being twelve (12) minutes with sudden death overtime. Offenses will have four (4) downs to gain ten (10) yards, and a first down will extend drives. Six (6) points are provided for every touchdown scored, and teams can attempt a one (1) point conversion after a touchdown from the five-yard line or a 2pt. conversion from the ten-yard line.

The game will feature a ten (10) yard endzones and a fifty (50) yard field.

The 7-on-7 format will feature offenses that include a Quarterback, Running Back, Center, two Wide Receivers, and two Tight Ends.

Defenses will feature a Middle Linebacker, two Safeties, two Defensive Ends, and two Cornerbacks.
